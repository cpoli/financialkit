financialkit.asset_pricing
==========================


.. include:: /_generated/nav/asset_pricing.rst

Williams's dividend discount model and Gordon growth (through
:func:`financialkit.time_value.perpetuity_present_value`),
Modigliani-Miller and the weighted average cost of capital,
Shiller's variance bound and the Campbell-Shiller log-linear present
value; Sharpe's CAPM with the market as the tangency
portfolio (:func:`financialkit.risk.safety_first_portfolio`), Jensen's
alpha, Black's zero-beta CAPM and the Gibbons-Ross-Shanken test;
time-series factor regressions (through
:func:`financialkit.time_series.ols`), Fama-MacBeth premia, Ross's APT
and its arbitrage portfolios, and the characteristic sorts behind the
Fama-French and Carhart factors, the five-factor model, and
Bonferroni, Holm and Benjamini-Yekutieli hurdles for the factor zoo;
Lucas's tree, Breeden's consumption CAPM, the Mehra-Prescott economy,
Campbell-Cochrane habit and Bansal-Yaron long-run risk; Harrison-Kreps
state prices with ``scipy.optimize.linprog`` and the
Hansen-Jagannathan bound; Hansen's GMM (``scipy.optimize.least_squares``
with Newey-West weighting) and the Hansen-Singleton Euler equation.
