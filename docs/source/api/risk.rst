financialkit.risk
=================


.. include:: /_generated/nav/risk.rst

Value at risk by historical simulation, normal and Student-t closed
forms, RiskMetrics delta-normal, the Cornish-Fisher expansion,
full-revaluation Monte Carlo through
:func:`financialkit.integrators.correlated_increments`,
Boudoukh-Richardson-Whitelaw age weighting, filtered historical
simulation, Rouvinez's exact delta-gamma VaR and
Bangia-Diebold-Schuermann-Stroughair liquidity-adjusted VaR; Roy's
safety-first criterion; Acerbi-Tasche expected shortfall with normal and
Student-t closed forms, the coherence axioms, Euler risk allocation and
the FRTB's liquidity-horizon ES; block maxima
(``scipy.stats.genextreme``) and peaks over threshold
(``scipy.stats.genpareto``); Kupiec and Christoffersen backtests, the
Basel traffic light and Acerbi-Szekely ES backtests; Gaussian and t
copulas with tail dependence; drawdowns and the Calmar ratio.
