financialkit.time_series
========================


.. include:: /_generated/nav/time_series.rst

Returns and their stylized facts (the Hill tail index, moments and the
Jarque-Bera test); tests of the random walk (serial correlation, Fama's
runs test, Box-Pierce and Ljung-Box, Lo-MacKinlay variance ratios);
Yule-Walker autoregressions (``scipy.linalg.solve_toeplitz``), partial
autocorrelations, ARMA simulation (``scipy.signal.lfilter``) and
conditional maximum likelihood (``scipy.optimize.least_squares``) with
AIC/BIC order selection; Sims's vector autoregressions with impulse
responses and variance decompositions; the augmented Dickey-Fuller, KPSS,
Engle-Granger and Johansen tests; Hurst's rescaled range and fractional
Gaussian noise; Kalman's filter and smoother with the local-level model;
Hamilton's Markov-switching model by EM; ARCH, GARCH, EGARCH, GJR-GARCH and
RiskMetrics EWMA volatility models fitted by maximum likelihood
(``scipy.optimize.minimize``), Engle's ARCH-LM test and DCC-GARCH
correlations; and Parkinson's range estimator, realized variance and
Corsi's HAR model.
