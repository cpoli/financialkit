financialkit.market_microstructure
==================================


.. include:: /_generated/nav/market_microstructure.rst

Demsetz's quoted, effective and realized spreads and the price impact;
the Roll and Corwin-Schultz spread estimators; Lee-Ready trade
classification with the tick test, the quote rule and lagged quotes;
Garman's Poisson dealer, Ho-Stoll inventory quotes and
Avellaneda-Stoikov market making, simulated around a Brownian mid price
from :func:`financialkit.integrators.brownian_increments`; Kyle's
insider and the Glosten-Milgrom dealer; Hasbrouck's trade-impact VAR;
PIN by maximum likelihood and VPIN by volume buckets; the square-root
impact law, Bertsimas-Lo dynamic programming, Almgren-Chriss
efficient execution and Obizhaeva-Wang transient impact; Amihud
illiquidity and Kyle's lambda by regression; Hawkes self-exciting order
flow, the Cont-Stoikov-Talreja order book and Cont-Kukanov-Stoikov
order-flow imbalance.
