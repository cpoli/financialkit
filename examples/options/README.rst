Examples
========

This gallery walks through every public feature of ``financialkit.options``:
the closed-form pricers from Bachelier to Garman-Kohlhagen, put-call
parity and the no-arbitrage bounds, the Greeks and delta hedging,
implied volatility, the smile and the risk-neutral density, and the
binomial and trinomial trees with American early exercise.

Each script in this gallery is self-contained and can be run directly with
``python examples/options/<section>/<script>.py``.

Sections
--------

- **closed_form** -- Bachelier, Stoll's put-call parity, Black-Scholes,
  Merton's dividends and bounds, Black-76 and Garman-Kohlhagen.
- **greeks** -- the Black-Scholes Greeks and discrete delta hedging.
- **implied_vol** -- implied volatility, the Breeden-Litzenberger density,
  the volatility smile and the Brenner-Subrahmanyam approximation.
- **trees** -- the Cox-Ross-Rubinstein, Rendleman-Bartter, Boyle
  trinomial and Leisen-Reimer lattices, and the Barone-Adesi-Whaley
  American approximation.
