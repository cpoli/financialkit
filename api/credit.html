financialkit.credit#

financialkit.credit hub · History · Examples · API reference

Altman’s Z-score and Fisher’s linear discriminant; Merton’s structural model through financialkit.options.black_scholes_price(), its calibration to equity and the Crosbie-Bohn distance to default; Black-Cox first passage and Leland’s endogenous default; piecewise-constant hazard curves, Jarrow-Turnbull bonds and the Duffie-Singleton, treasury and face-value recovery conventions (with a CIR intensity through financialkit.interest_rate_models.CIRModel); O’Kane-Turnbull CDS legs, par spreads, upfronts and the ISDA-style hazard bootstrap; CreditMetrics migration and credit VaR, Jarrow-Lando-Turnbull generator matrices (scipy.linalg.expm and logm) and the Israel-Rosenthal-Wei regularization; Vasicek’s large homogeneous portfolio, CreditRisk+ by Panjer’s recursion, Li’s Gaussian copula (through financialkit.risk.gaussian_copula_sample()), the Basel II IRB formula and Hull-White tranche pricing.

financialkit.credit: credit scoring, structural and reduced-form default models, CDS, rating migration and portfolio credit risk.

Altman’s Z-score; Merton’s structural model, its calibration to equity and Crosbie-Bohn distance to default (through financialkit.options.black_scholes_price()); Black-Cox first passage and Leland’s endogenous default; Jarrow-Turnbull hazard curves and Duffie-Singleton recovery of market value; O’Kane-Turnbull CDS legs, par spreads, upfronts and the ISDA-style hazard bootstrap; CreditMetrics migration, Jarrow-Lando-Turnbull generators (scipy.linalg.expm()) and Israel-Rosenthal-Wei regularization; Vasicek’s large homogeneous portfolio, CreditRisk+ by Panjer’s recursion, Li’s Gaussian copula, the Basel II IRB formula and Hull-White tranche pricing. Time is in years, rates are continuously compounded and recovery is a fraction of face value.
