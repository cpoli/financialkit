financialkit.time_value#

financialkit.time_value hub · History · Examples · API reference

Compounding at any frequency and equivalent rates; the rule of 72 and compound-interest tables; present value, net present value and Fisher’s real rate; annuities, perpetuities, life annuities and sinking funds; the internal rate of return (scipy.optimize.brentq), every root of the NPV polynomial (numpy.roots) and the modified IRR; amortization schedules and the Truth-in-Lending APR; day counts, year fractions and accrued interest for every DayCount convention.

financialkit.time_value: the time value of money.

Compounding at any frequency and the equivalence of rates (continuous by default); Pacioli’s rule of 72 and Witt’s compound-interest tables; present value, Fisher’s net present value and real rate of interest; annuities, perpetuities, De Witt’s life annuities and Price’s sinking fund; the internal rate of return (scipy.optimize.brentq()), every root of the NPV polynomial (numpy.roots()) with a warning when there are several, and Baldwin’s modified IRR; level-payment amortization schedules and the Truth-in-Lending APR; day counts, year fractions and accrued interest for every DayCount convention.

The year-fraction functions here are the package’s single implementation; later domains import them rather than redefining them.

exception financialkit.time_value.MultipleIRRWarning[source]#

Bases: UserWarning

Raised when a cash-flow stream has more than one internal rate of return.

By Descartes’ rule of signs a stream with more than one sign change can have several IRRs (Lorie and Savage, 1955), and then no single one of them is a meaningful rate of return.

financialkit.time_value.de_witt_survival(age, times, base_hazard=0.01)[source]#

Survival probabilities under De Witt’s (1671) mortality hypothesis.

De Witt assumed that between ages 3 and 53 the chance of dying is the same in every half-year, and that it is 1.5, 2 and 3 times as large in the bands 53-63, 63-73 and 73-80, with no one living beyond 80. Here this is a piecewise-constant force of mortality base_hazard * w.

Parameters:

  • age (float) – Age of the annuitant today, at least 3.

  • times (array-like) – Years from today.

  • base_hazard (float) – Annual force of mortality in the youngest band.

Return type:

ndarray

Returns:

ndarray – Probability of being alive at each time.

Examples

>>> de_witt_survival(70.0, [0.0, 5.0, 9.0, 10.0]).round(4).tolist()
[1.0, 0.8869, 0.7866, 0.0]

financialkit.time_value.rule_of_72(rate, numerator=72.0)[source]#

Pacioli’s rule: money doubles in about 72 / (100 * rate) years.

The rule approximates the exact annual-compounding doubling time \(\ln 2 / \ln(1 + r)\); 72 rather than \(100 \ln 2 \approx 69.3\) corrects for the curvature of \(\ln(1 + r)\) near 8%, and 72 has many divisors. Use numerator=69.3 for continuous compounding.

Parameters:

  • rate (float or array-like) – Annual rate as a decimal.

  • numerator (float) – The rule’s constant.

Returns:

float or ndarray

Examples

>>> rule_of_72(0.06)
12.0
