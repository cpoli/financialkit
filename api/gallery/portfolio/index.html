Examples#

This gallery walks through every public feature of financialkit.portfolio: Markowitz’s frontier, Tobin’s tangency portfolio, Merton’s analytic frontier and the 1/N benchmark; Kelly betting, Merton’s portfolio problem and CPPI; single-index and Ledoit-Wolf covariance estimates; the Sharpe and Sortino ratios and Grinold’s fundamental law; Black-Litterman and Michaud resampling; and CVaR optimization, maximum diversification, risk parity and hierarchical risk parity.

Each script in this gallery is self-contained and can be run directly with python examples/portfolio/<section>/<script>.py.

Sections#

  • mean_variance – the Markowitz frontier, Tobin separation, Merton’s closed form and DeMiguel-Garlappi-Uppal’s 1/N.

  • growth – the Kelly criterion, Merton’s portfolio problem and CPPI.

  • covariance – Sharpe’s single-index model and Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage.

  • performance – the Sharpe ratio, Grinold’s fundamental law and the Sortino ratio.

  • bayesian – Black-Litterman and Michaud’s resampled frontier.

  • risk_based – CVaR optimization, the most-diversified portfolio, risk parity and hierarchical risk parity.
