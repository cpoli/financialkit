Examples#

This gallery walks through every public feature of financialkit.asset_pricing: equity valuation by discounted dividends, Modigliani-Miller and Shiller’s variance bound; the CAPM, Jensen’s alpha, the zero-beta CAPM and the GRS test; factor regressions, Fama-MacBeth, the APT, Fama-French and Carhart factors; consumption-based pricing and the equity premium puzzle; and the stochastic discount factor.

Each script in this gallery is self-contained and can be run directly with python examples/asset_pricing/<section>/<script>.py.

Sections#

  • valuation – Williams’s dividend discount model, Modigliani-Miller, Gordon growth and Shiller’s excess volatility.

  • capm – the security market line, Jensen’s alpha, Black’s zero-beta CAPM and the Gibbons-Ross-Shanken test.

  • factors – Fama-MacBeth regressions, Ross’s APT, the Fama-French three factors and Carhart’s momentum.

  • consumption – Lucas’s tree, Breeden’s consumption CAPM and the equity premium puzzle.

  • sdf – Harrison-Kreps state prices and the Hansen-Jagannathan bound.
