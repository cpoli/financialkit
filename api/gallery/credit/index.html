Examples#

This gallery walks through every public feature of financialkit.credit: Altman’s Z-score; Merton’s structural model, Black-Cox first passage, Leland’s endogenous default and the KMV distance to default; hazard rates and recovery conventions; CDS pricing and hazard bootstrapping; rating migration with CreditMetrics and generator matrices; and portfolio credit risk with Vasicek’s large pool, CreditRisk+, Li’s copula, the Basel IRB formula and CDO tranches.

See also the narrative tutorial:

  • Credit Risk, from One Firm to a Portfolio

Each script in this gallery is self-contained and can be run directly with python examples/credit/<section>/<script>.py.

Sections#

  • scoring – Altman’s Z-score.

  • structural – Merton, Black-Cox, Leland and the distance to default.

  • reduced_form – Jarrow-Turnbull hazard rates and Duffie-Singleton recovery of market value.

  • cds – the CDS par spread and the ISDA hazard bootstrap.

  • migration – CreditMetrics, generator matrices and the Israel-Rosenthal-Wei regularization.

  • portfolio_loss – Vasicek’s large pool, CreditRisk+, Li’s Gaussian copula, the Basel IRB formula and tranche expected losses.
