Examples#

This gallery walks through every public feature of financialkit.market_microstructure: spread measures and estimators, trade classification, dealer and market-making models, asymmetric information and informed-trading measures, optimal execution and illiquidity.

Each script in this gallery is self-contained and can be run directly with python examples/market_microstructure/<section>/<script>.py.

Sections#

  • spreads – Demsetz’s spreads, Roll’s estimator, Lee-Ready trade classification and the Corwin-Schultz high-low estimator.

  • dealers – Garman’s Poisson dealer, Ho-Stoll inventory quotes and Avellaneda-Stoikov market making.

  • information – Kyle’s insider, Glosten-Milgrom, Hasbrouck’s trade-impact VAR, PIN and VPIN.

  • execution – the square-root impact law, Bertsimas-Lo and Almgren-Chriss.

  • liquidity – Amihud’s illiquidity ratio.
