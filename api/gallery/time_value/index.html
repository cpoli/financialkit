Examples#

This gallery walks through every public feature of financialkit.time_value: compounding and equivalent rates, present and net present value, annuities and perpetuities, the internal rate of return and its pitfalls, amortizing loans and the APR, and day-count conventions.

Each script in this gallery is self-contained and can be run directly with python examples/time_value/<section>/<script>.py.

Sections#

  • present_value – Fibonacci’s present value, Fisher’s real rate and Fisher’s net present value.

  • compounding – Pacioli’s rule of 72, Witt’s compound-interest tables and Bernoulli’s continuous compounding.

  • annuities – De Witt’s life annuities, the consol perpetuity and Price’s sinking fund.

  • irr – the internal rate of return, Lorie and Savage’s multiple IRRs and Baldwin’s modified IRR.

  • amortization – the fully amortizing mortgage and the Truth-in-Lending APR.

  • day_counts – 30/360 accrued interest and the actual/actual family.
