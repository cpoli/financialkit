Because every bond price is linear in d, it is also linear in the coefficients, so the fit is ordinary least squares on a cross-section of bond prices. His 1971 paper used quadratic splines; the cubic version (1975) gives smooth forward rates.">

Solving that system one bond at a time, shortest first, is bootstrapping: each bond adds one zero rate at its maturity. With more bonds than dates the same system is solved by least squares.">
