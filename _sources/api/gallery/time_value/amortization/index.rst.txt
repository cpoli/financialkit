Amortization
------------

Level-payment loans that repay themselves, and the APR that the Truth
in Lending Act made lenders disclose.

The Home Owners&#x27; Loan Corporation (1933) and then the FHA (1934) replaced it with the long-term, level-payment, fully amortizing mortgage: each monthly payment covers the interest on the balance and repays a little principal, so the loan is gone at the last payment.">

The APR therefore rises above the note rate when fees are charged, and the faster the loan is repaid, the more the fees count. The APR is still a nominal rate; compounded monthly, the effective annual rate is higher again.">
