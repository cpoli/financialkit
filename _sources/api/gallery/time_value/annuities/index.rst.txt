Annuities
---------

Streams of level payments: De Witt's life annuities, the perpetual
consol, and Price's sinking fund.

He valued an annuity as the sum of each payment, discounted at 4% and weighted by the chance that the buyer is still alive to receive it, under a simple mortality hypothesis: an equal chance of death in every half-year up to age 53, rising to 1.5, 2 and 3 times that in the bands 53-63, 63-73 and 73-80.">

A perpetuity paying P is worth P/i at yield i, so a consol&#x27;s price moves inversely with long-term rates and at a 3% yield it trades at par. This script prices consols across yields and compares them with long but finite annuities.">
