Over the same period stocks earned 6.98% a year and short-term bills 0.80%: a premium of 6.18%. Searching over every time preference \beta &lt; 1 and risk aversion \gamma \le 10, they could produce a premium of at most 0.35% while keeping the riskless rate below 4%. Consumption is simply too smooth for stocks to be that risky to a power-utility agent.">
