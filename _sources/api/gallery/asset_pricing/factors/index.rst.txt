Factor models
-------------

Estimating risk premia from the cross-section, the arbitrage argument
for linear pricing, and factors built by sorting stocks on size, value
and momentum.

If returns follow a factor model r_i = E[r_i] + \beta_i&#x27; f + \varepsilon_i with diversifiable \varepsilon_i, then any asset off the plane E[r_i] = r_f + \beta_i&#x27;\lambda can be combined with others into a portfolio with no cost and no factor risk that still earns a positive expected return. In a large market its idiosyncratic risk diversifies away, and the portfolio becomes an arbitrage. The pricing errors therefore have to be small for all but finitely many assets.">
