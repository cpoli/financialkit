Closed-form pricers
-------------------

European option prices in closed form, from Bachelier's normal model to
Black-Scholes-Merton and its forward, futures and currency variants, and
the model-free relations every price must obey.

In his model price changes are normal with an absolute volatility \sigma_N, so an at-the-money option is worth \sigma_N\sqrt{T/(2\pi)}: the square-root-of-time law. Below, the formula is checked against simulated arithmetic Brownian paths and compared with Black-Scholes at a matched volatility.">
