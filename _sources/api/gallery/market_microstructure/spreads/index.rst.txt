Spreads
-------

The price of immediacy, measured from quotes, from trades, and from prices alone.

.. raw:: html

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="Harold Demsetz (1968) argued that a trader who wants to trade now pays the dealer the spread for standing ready, and that the spread shrinks as trading activity grows, because a busy dealer turns over inventory faster. Today the spread is measured three ways: the quoted spread on the screen, the effective spread actually paid relative to the midquote, and the realized spread the liquidity provider keeps once the price has moved; the difference is the price impact, lost to better-informed traders.">

Shane Corwin and Paul Schultz (2012) noticed that the volatility part of the squared range doubles over two days while the spread part does not, and solved for the spread from two one-day ranges and one two-day range. It needs only daily data, yet is far more precise than Roll&#x27;s estimator from daily closes.">
