Stochastic volatility
---------------------

Volatility with its own randomness: Hull and White's mixing formula for
uncorrelated variance, and Heston's correlated square-root model.

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="Hull and White (1987) let the variance follow its own lognormal diffusion, dV = \xi V\,dW, independent of the stock. Conditional on the variance path the stock is lognormal with the path&#x27;s average variance \bar V, so the option price is the Black-Scholes price averaged over the distribution of \bar V: the mixing formula. They expanded it in a series in \xi^2 T; here it is also computed by simulating variance paths. Because the variance is uncorrelated with the stock the smile is symmetric, and the at-the-money price falls below Black-Scholes, which is concave in the variance there.">
