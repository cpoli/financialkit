Compound, forward-start and chooser options
-------------------------------------------

Options whose payoff is itself an option: Geske's options on options,
Rubinstein's forward-start options and choosers.

His motivation was corporate finance: if equity is a call on the firm&#x27;s assets, a stock option is a call on a call, and its implied volatility should rise as the stock falls and leverage grows. The compound call is exercised at T_1 only if the underlying option is worth more than the compound strike, which happens above a critical spot I, and the joint law of the price at T_1 and T_2 brings in the bivariate normal distribution with correlation \sqrt{T_1/T_2}.">
