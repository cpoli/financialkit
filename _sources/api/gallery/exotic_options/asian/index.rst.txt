Asian options
-------------

Options on an average price: the geometric closed form, the arithmetic
average by Monte Carlo with the geometric control variate, and
lognormal moment matching.

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="An average-rate option pays on the average price over its life, which makes it cheaper than a vanilla option and harder to manipulate at expiry. Angelien Kemna and Ton Vorst (1990) observed that a geometric average of lognormal prices is lognormal, with volatility \sigma/\sqrt3 under continuous averaging, so its option has a Black-Scholes formula. The arithmetic average traded in practice has none, but the geometric option is so tightly correlated with it that it makes a near-perfect Monte Carlo control variate.">
