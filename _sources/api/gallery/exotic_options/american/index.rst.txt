American options by simulation
------------------------------

Longstaff and Schwartz's least-squares Monte Carlo for early exercise.

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="Monte Carlo simulates forward, but early exercise is decided backward: whether to exercise today depends on the value of waiting. Francis Longstaff and Eduardo Schwartz (2001) estimated that continuation value by regressing the discounted future cash flows of the in-the-money paths on a few functions of today&#x27;s price. Working back from expiry, each path is exercised where the payoff beats the fitted value. The method extends to many assets and path-dependent payoffs, where trees and PDEs give up.">
