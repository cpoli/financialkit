Binary options
--------------

All-or-nothing payoffs, the building blocks into which Reiner and
Rubinstein decomposed vanilla and barrier options.

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="Mark Rubinstein and Eric Reiner&#x27;s &quot;Unscrambling the Binary Code&quot; (1991) priced the all-or-nothing payoffs and showed that every vanilla and barrier option is assembled from them. A cash-or-nothing call pays 1 if S_T &gt; K and is worth e^{-rT}N(d_2); an asset-or-nothing call pays the share and is worth Se^{-qT}N(d_1). A vanilla call is one asset-or-nothing call minus K cash-or-nothing calls. A gap option uses different trigger and payoff strikes and can be worth less than nothing, and a one-touch pays as soon as a barrier is hit.">
