    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="William Margrabe (1978) priced the right to exchange one asset for another, (S_1 - S_2)^+. Measured in units of the second asset, it is a call on the ratio S_1/S_2 struck at 1, so the interest rate drops out and only the volatility of the ratio, \sigma^2 = \sigma_1^2 + \sigma_2^2 - 2\rho\sigma_1\sigma_2, matters. Change of numeraire, the idea behind this one-line proof, became one of the central tools of derivatives pricing.">

The answer needs the bivariate normal distribution. Because \max + \min = S_1 + S_2, the two calls always add up to the two vanilla calls, and correlation moves value between them: diversification makes the maximum more valuable and the minimum less.">
