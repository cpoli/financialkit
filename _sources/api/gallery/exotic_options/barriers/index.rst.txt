Barrier options
---------------

Options that die or come alive when the price touches a level: Merton's
image solution, the Reiner-Rubinstein family of closed forms, double
barriers and the correction for barriers checked only at discrete dates.

Every one is a sum of six building blocks A, \dots, F: the vanilla option, the vanilla struck at the barrier, their reflections through the barrier and the two rebates. Without rebates, a knock-in and a knock-out with the same terms add up to the vanilla option.">

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="A double knock-out dies if the price leaves a corridor (L, U). Naoto Ikeda and Masaaki Kunitomo (1992) solved it, even for curved (exponential) barriers, with an infinite series of images reflected alternately through both boundaries. The terms decay like e^{-2n^2\ln^2(U/L)/\sigma^2T}, so two or three are plenty unless the corridor is very narrow. Unlike a vanilla call, a double knock-out call loses value as volatility rises: more paths hit a barrier.">
