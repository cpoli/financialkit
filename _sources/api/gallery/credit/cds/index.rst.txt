    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="A credit default swap exchanges a running premium for a payment of 1 - R at default. O&#x27;Kane and Turnbull (2003) wrote both legs as integrals over the default density and evaluated them on a grid with defaults at mid-step, including the premium accrued between the last payment date and default. The par spread equates the legs; for a flat hazard and a continuously paid premium it is exactly \lambda(1 - R).">
