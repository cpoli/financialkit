Short-rate models
-----------------

Models of the instantaneous short rate that produce their own yield
curve: Merton, Vasicek and Cox-Ingersoll-Ross, the affine family that
contains them, and the two-factor G2++, all checked against Feynman-Kac
Monte Carlo.
