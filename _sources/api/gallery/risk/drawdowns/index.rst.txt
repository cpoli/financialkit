    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="A drawdown is the fall from the highest wealth reached so far. Terry Young (1991) proposed judging managed-futures funds by their compound annual return divided by their maximum drawdown, the Calmar ratio, because investors leave a fund after a deep drawdown long before its volatility looks alarming. Two strategies with the same mean and volatility can have very different drawdowns if their bad days cluster.">
