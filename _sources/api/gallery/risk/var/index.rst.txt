Value at risk
-------------

The loss that is exceeded with a given small probability, estimated in
closed form, by expansion, by simulation and from weighted or filtered
history.

For options that is badly wrong: a short straddle has zero delta at the money, so its delta-normal VaR is zero, yet it loses heavily on a large move in either direction.
