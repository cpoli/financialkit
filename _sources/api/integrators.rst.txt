financialkit.integrators
==========================

Shared SDE path simulation used by every domain that needs paths:
Euler-Maruyama and Milstein schemes, exact geometric Brownian motion and
Ornstein-Uhlenbeck sampling, plain, correlated (Cholesky), antithetic
and scrambled-Sobol Brownian increments, Brownian-bridge construction,
and empirical strong and weak convergence orders.
